# 📌 Unit-wise Notes (Theory)

## 🔹 Unit I – Basics of AI & Problem Solving Techniques

This unit introduces the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence, including its scope, applications, and various types of AI systems. It covers intelligent agents, rational behavior, and the Turing Test used to evaluate machine intelligence. Students will also learn classical and heuristic search techniques such as BFS, DFS, Hill Climbing, Greedy Search, A* Search, and problem-solving strategies in AI.

📌 **Mapped CO:** CO1

---

## 🔹 Unit II – Knowledge & Reasoning

This unit focuses on how knowledge is represented, stored, and utilized by intelligent systems. It covers knowledge-based agents, propositional and first-order logic, inference mechanisms, and reasoning techniques. Students will also explore uncertainty handling in AI through probabilistic reasoning and Bayes' Theorem.

📌 **Mapped CO:** CO2

---

## 🔹 Unit III – Introduction to Machine Learning

This unit provides an overview of Machine Learning concepts, its history, and its relationship with Artificial Intelligence. It explains the Machine Learning life cycle, different forms of data, data preparation techniques, and dataset management. The unit also introduces data cleaning methods such as handling missing values and outliers to improve model performance.

📌 **Mapped CO:** CO3

---

## 🔹 Unit IV – Types of Learning

This unit introduces the major learning paradigms used in Machine Learning, including supervised, unsupervised, and semi-supervised learning. Students will study popular algorithms such as Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest, K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), and K-Means Clustering. The unit also covers model validation using cross-validation techniques.

📌 **Mapped CO:** CO4

---

## 🔹 Unit V – Regression & Classification

This unit focuses on predictive modeling techniques used for regression and classification tasks. It covers simple and multiple linear regression, logistic regression, overfitting and underfitting concepts, and performance evaluation metrics. Students will learn to assess model accuracy using confusion matrices, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC curves, and other standard measures.

📌 **Mapped CO:** CO5
