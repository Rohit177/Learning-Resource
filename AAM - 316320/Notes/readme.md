# 📌 Unit-wise Notes (Theory)

## 🔹 Unit I – ML Models & Feature Engineering

This unit introduces Machine Learning models and the process of training supervised learning algorithms. It focuses on understanding data, extracting useful features, and applying feature engineering techniques to numerical, categorical, and text data. Students also learn the importance of feature scaling and feature selection for improving model performance.

📌 **Mapped CO:** CO1

---

## 🔹 Unit II – Supervised & Unsupervised Learning Algorithms

This unit covers advanced Machine Learning algorithms used for classification, clustering, dimensionality reduction, and pattern discovery. Students learn Support Vector Machines (SVM), K-Medoids clustering, Principal Component Analysis (PCA), Association Rule Learning algorithms, and modern Generative Models such as GANs and VAEs.

📌 **Mapped CO:** CO2

---

## 🔹 Unit III – Artificial Neural Networks

This unit introduces the fundamentals of Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) and their role in Machine Learning. It covers perceptron learning, training rules, gradient descent optimization techniques, and commonly used activation functions such as Sigmoid, ReLU, Tanh, and Softmax for neural network training.

📌 **Mapped CO:** CO3

---

## 🔹 Unit IV – Convolutional Neural Networks

This unit focuses on Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) used for image processing and computer vision applications. Students learn CNN architecture, convolution operations, padding, pooling, and strided convolution. The unit also explores popular CNN architectures including LeNet, AlexNet, VGGNet, ResNet, and GoogleNet through case studies.

📌 **Mapped CO:** CO4

---

## 🔹 Unit V – Deep Learning for Sequential & Image Data

This unit covers advanced Deep Learning techniques for handling sequential and image-based data. It introduces Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), LSTMs, and GRUs for sequence modeling, along with transfer learning and pre-trained models for image classification. The unit concludes with an introduction to Transformers and Generative Pre-trained Transformers (GPT).

📌 **Mapped CO:** CO5
